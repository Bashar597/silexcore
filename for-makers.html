What you can do

Projects that actually work.

01
Paper Circuits

Draw functional circuits on plain paper or cardboard. Light LEDs, build interactive art, prototype wearables. No soldering, no etching — just paint.

02
Remote Repair

Worn rubber button pads on your TV remote? A dot of Conductive Silicone restores the contact. 30 minutes of work vs. buying a new remote.

03
Guitar Shielding

Coat pickup cavities with Premium Conductive Paint to eliminate 60 Hz hum. DIY-able in an afternoon. Dramatically quieter signal.

04
PCB Repair

Cracked PCB trace? Conductive Paint or Glue bridges it without heat. Ideal for single-sided boards, flex cables, and heat-sensitive assemblies.

05
E-Textiles

Conductive Silicone applied to fabric stays flexible through wash cycles. Build pressure sensors, touch interfaces, or light-up wearables.

06
Rapid Prototyping

Skip the etching tank. Paint traces on your substrate, test same day, revise. Ideal for Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and sensor projects.
