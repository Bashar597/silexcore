Every SilexCore product ships with a complete Safety Data Sheet classified under OSHA 29 CFR 1910.1200 and GHS. Required for any regulated lab environment — and we provide it without asking.

Research depends on repeatability. Our formulations are batch-tested and conductivity ranges are published. If your study requires a certificate of analysis, contact us — we provide them for institutional orders.

Research Applications

Where we show up in the lab.

01
Sensor Fabrication

Build custom pressure, strain, and touch sensors using conductive silicone traces. Widely used in materials science and biomedical engineering research.

02
STEM Education

Safe for students from Grade 6 onward. Used in physics, chemistry, and engineering courses to demonstrate conductivity, circuit design, and materials science.

03
EMI/EMF Studies

The Premium Conductive Paint's 54 dB attenuation is reproducible and documentable — suitable for RF shielding effectiveness studies and Faraday cage experiments.

04
Prototype Circuits

Rapid prototyping without photolithography or etching. Apply, test, revise — same day. Used in EE departments for quick-iteration circuit research.

05
Solar Cell Research

Conductive Glue for tab bonding on photovoltaic cells. Heat-free application protects silicon wafers from thermal stress during assembly.

06
Wearable Tech

Conductive Silicone maintains electrical properties through bending and stretching. Used in wearable sensor research and e-textile development programs.
