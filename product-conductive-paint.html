SilexCore Conductive Paint

Your All-in-One Solution for Circuits, Repair, and EMF Shielding

0.1–0.5 Ω/sq Water-Based Low-VOC Engineered in USA

Tired of complicated electronics or messy soldering? SilexCore Conductive Paint is a highly conductive, non-toxic, water-based formula that works like a "liquid wire" — letting you draw circuits, repair damaged PCB traces, and shield electronics from EMF without a single solder joint. Whether you're a student building your first circuit, a maker repairing a guitar cavity, or an engineer prototyping a new design, this paint makes conductivity accessible, clean, and fast.

What Makes It Different

  • Versatile Circuitry & PCB Repair

    Use it for everything from delicate PCB trace repairs to complex electronic art and sensor prototyping. Bridge a broken trace, draw a new circuit path, or bond a lifted pad — all without heat or flux. It's the ultimate liquid wire for any project.

  • Safe, Non-Toxic & Eco-Friendly

    Our water-based formula is 100% non-toxic and free of heavy metals, making it the safe choice for homes, schools, and professional workshops. Clean up with just soap and water before the paint cures — no solvents, no fumes, no PPE required.

  • Fast-Drying & Simple Application

    No special tools required. The paint becomes touch-dry in about 30 minutes at room temperature and reaches full electrical conductivity once completely cured (typically 24 hours). Apply with the precision pen tip, a brush, or screen-print it — your choice.

  • Universal Bonding Across Multiple Surfaces

    Engineered to adhere to almost anything — paper, cardboard, wood, drywall, glass, most plastics, and metals. The formula forms a durable conductive film that stays put without peeling or cracking under normal indoor use conditions.

  • High-Performance EMF & EMI Shielding

    Provides reliable conductivity and strong EMI shielding performance. Multiple coats increase attenuation — a single layer delivers up to 40 dB, two layers reach 48 dB, and three layers achieve 54 dB of EMF shielding effectiveness. Widely used for guitar cavity shielding to eliminate hum and noise.

Applications

  • Printed Electronic Circuits: Create functional circuits directly on paper, cardboard, fabric, or PCB without any soldering.
  • PCB Trace Repair: Fix damaged or broken circuit traces quickly and efficiently — no rework station needed.
  • Electronic Prototyping: Develop and test new circuit designs without complex manufacturing or etching processes.
  • EMF & EMI Shielding: Reduce electromagnetic interference and protect sensitive electronics, instruments, and enclosures.
  • Guitar & Instrument Shielding: Line cavity interiors to eliminate single-coil hum and noise pickup.
  • STEM Education & DIY: Perfect for classroom projects, hands-on learning, and interactive electronics experiments.

Compatible Surfaces

  • Paper and cardboard
  • Plastic and glass
  • Wood and drywall
  • Metals and coated surfaces

Usage Guidelines

  • Designed for indoor use only
  • Apply on clean, dry surfaces for optimal conductivity
  • Allow full drying time before testing circuits or measuring resistance
  • Multiple thin coats produce better results than one thick coat
