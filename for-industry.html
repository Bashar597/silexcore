What we offer industry

More than a product.
A supply partner.

01

Bulk Supply

Standard formulations available in production quantities. Consistent batch-to-batch conductivity with lot documentation. Volume pricing tiered at 10, 50, and 100+ unit orders.

02

Custom Formulations

Need a specific viscosity, conductivity target, cure schedule, or substrate compatibility? We develop custom formulations under NDA for qualified production partners.

03

Private Label

Sell SilexCore materials under your own brand. We offer white-label and private-label programs for distributors and OEM integrators. Minimum order quantities apply.

Industries Served

Where our materials go to work.

01
Electronics Manufacturing

PCB repair, trace restoration, and solder-free bonding in production environments. Compatible with automated dispensing systems.

02
EMI Shielding

High-attenuation shielding coatings for enclosures, housings, and panels. Up to 54 dB at 1 GHz. Indoor and outdoor rated.

03
Automotive & Sensors

Sensor repair and fabrication without soldering. Water-resistant formulations for underhood and exterior applications.

04
Medical Devices

Biocompatibility documentation available on request. Used in prototype medical sensor assemblies where heat-free bonding is required.

05
Solar & Energy

Tab bonding for photovoltaic cell assembly. Conductive adhesive that eliminates thermal stress on silicon during production.

06
Wearable Technology

Flexible conductive traces for e-textile and wearable production lines. Maintains conductivity through repeated flex, wash, and compression cycles.

How to get started

The OEM process.

01
Initial Inquiry
Send us your application requirements, volumes, and timeline via the contact form. We'll respond within one business day.
02
NDA & Technical Brief
For custom formulations, we sign an NDA and collect a technical brief covering your conductivity specs, substrate, cure process, and environment.
03
Pilot Run
A sample or small pilot run ships so you can validate in your process. We iterate until the spec is met.
04
Production Supply
Ongoing production supply with lot certificates, scheduled delivery, and dedicated account support.
