SC-110CS

Conductive Silicone for
EMI Shielding, Remote & Keypad Repair

Flexible, Waterproof — For Surfaces That Move and Get Wet

5–35 Ω/sq Waterproof Flexible Indoor / Outdoor

Silexcore Conductive Silicone (SC-110CS )is a versatile solution for EMI shielding, remote control button repair, and restoring conductive rubber keypads. This electrically conductive silicone combines reliable conductivity - sheet resistance ranging from 5Ω/sq to 35Ω/sq - with the flexibility and durability of silicone, making it ideal for repairing electronic devices, sealing components, and creating flexible conductive traces. Designed for both indoor and outdoor use, the material is waterproof, helping protect electronic assemblies from moisture while maintaining stable electrical performance. It offers a practical and dependable way to fix unresponsive buttons, support electronics projects, and develop custom circuits or sensor applications.

What Makes SilexCore Conductive Silicone Different

Silexcore Conductive Silicone provides reliable electrical conductivity with the flexibility of silicone, making it ideal for electronics repair, EMI shielding, and flexible circuit applications. The following features highlight its key performance benefits:-

  • Stays Conductive Through Flex & Stretch

    Where conductive paint would crack and fail after a few bending cycles, SC-110CS maintains its conductivity through repeated flexing and stretching. The silicone base moves with the substrate — whether it's a rubber keypad, a stretch fabric, or a silicone sheet — without forming micro-fractures that break the conductive path. Sheet resistance of 5–35 Ω/sq is maintained even after extended mechanical cycling..

  • Durable and Waterproof Conductive Silicone for Electronics

    Unlike water-based conductive paints that can absorb moisture and lose conductivity when wet, SC-110CS cures to a fully waterproof silicone elastomer. Joints are sealed against water ingress, humidity, and brief immersion. This makes it ideal for outdoor sensor installations, marine electronics, wearable devices, and any application where water exposure is unavoidable.

  • Restores Worn Rubber Keypad Contacts

    Rubber keypads — on TV remotes, industrial controls, medical devices, and automotive panels — wear out in a predictable way: the conductive carbon coating on the rubber dome degrades, and buttons stop registering. SC-110CS can restore these contacts precisely. Apply a small dot to the worn rubber contact, allow it to cure 24 hours, and the keypad functions like new. This repair is significantly cheaper than a replacement unit and takes minutes to perform.

  • Conductive Silicone for Circuit Prototyping Without Acid Etching

    SC-110CS provides a practical alternative to traditional acid etching methods when creating experimental circuits. It allows engineers, makers, and hobbyists to quickly prototype conductive paths without complex chemical processes.

  • Conductive Silicone for EMI Shielding

    SC-110CS provides EMI shielding for silicone-based enclosures and flexible electronic assemblies while sealing and bonding components without compromising electrical conductivity.

  • Easy to Apply with No Mixing, No Solvents

    SC-110CS is a single-part compound — no mixing ratios, no pot life to race against, no solvents to handle. Apply directly from the precision tube tip to your substrate, shape as needed, and let it cure at room temperature. The fine tip allows precise application to small contacts, narrow traces, and tight repair areas without flooding adjacent components, also can be cleaned or removed before curing using isopropyl alcohol or acetone.

Applications for SilexCore Conductive Silicone

  • Remote & Keypad Contact Restoration: Repair worn rubber dome contacts on remotes, game controllers, electronic keypads, industrial panels, and medical devices — no replacement part needed.
  • Flexible Circuit Prototyping: Prototype circuits on silicone, rubber, and flex substrates without acid etching or photolithography.
  • Wearable Electronics & E-Textiles: Create conductive traces on stretch fabric and silicone for wearable sensors, health monitors, and interactive garments.
  • Waterproof Conductive Sealing: Seal and electrically bond joints on outdoor electronics, simultaneously creating a watertight connection.
  • EMI shielding: EMI shielding for silicone-based enclosures and flexible electronics.
  • Marine & Outdoor Electronics: Bond and seal electrical connections exposed to weather, spray, and condensation.
  • Circuit Boards: Restoring damaged conductive traces on circuit boards.

Compatible Surfaces

  • Rubber and silicone
  • flexible substrates
  • PCB and glass
  • Metal, cardboard, paper, wood, drywall and most plastics

Usage Guidelines

  • Rated for both indoor and outdoor use after cure - offering reliable electrical performance in various environments.
  • Apply to clean, dry surfaces — remove oils and surface contamination before use
  • Full cure at 24 hours room temperature; thin applications cure faster
  • No mixing or solvents required — single-part, ready to use
