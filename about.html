Our Story

Built on real materials science.
Not marketing.

SilexCore Technologies was founded with a single premise: the world needs conductive materials that actually work, come with real documentation, and are made by people who stand behind them.

Every product in our lineup is engineered and manufactured in Charlotte, North Carolina. We're not a reseller, and we're not a pour-and-relabel operation. We develop our own formulations using nanotechnology-enhanced materials and test each batch against published conductivity specifications.

From the student building their first LED paper circuit to the engineer specifying a shielding compound for a production run — we make products that work for both, with the documentation to prove it.

What We Stand For

Three things
we don't compromise on.

01

Honest Specifications

We publish real conductivity numbers — sheet resistance ranges, cure times, and operating conditions — measured under real conditions. No marketing ranges that only apply in ideal lab setups.

02

Safety Documentation

Every SilexCore product ships with a full OSHA/GHS Safety Data Sheet. We don't sell anything we can't document completely. Safe for classrooms, labs, workshops, and production environments.

03

Made in the USA

Designed, formulated, and manufactured in Charlotte, North Carolina. We believe in domestic manufacturing — for quality control, for traceability, and because we think where things are made still matters.
