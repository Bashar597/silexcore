SC-700PCP

SilexCore Premium Conductive Paint

Maximum Conductivity. Professional-Grade EMF & EMI Shielding.

54 dB Shielding Water-Resistant Indoor / Outdoor Engineered in USA

When standard conductivity isn't enough, SC-700PCP is the answer. SilexCore Premium Conductive Paint is a nanotechnology-enhanced, water-resistant formula engineered to deliver the highest electromagnetic shielding performance in our lineup — 54 dB of peak EMF/EMI attenuation. Whether you're lining a guitar cavity, shielding an RF-sensitive enclosure, or protecting outdoor electronics, this is the formulation that professionals reach for when performance is non-negotiable.

What Makes It Different

  • 54 dB Peak EMF/EMI Attenuation

    The highest shielding performance in the SilexCore range. Each additional coat increases attenuation — a single layer delivers 40 dB, two layers reach 48 dB, and three layers achieve a full 54 dB. That equates to a reduction factor of over 250,000, blocking 99.9996% of electromagnetic interference.

  • Nanotechnology-Enhanced Formula

    SC-700PCP uses an advanced silver-carbon nanoparticle suspension that produces a denser, more uniform conductive film than conventional graphite-based paints. The result is more shielding per coat, better adhesion on difficult surfaces, and a smoother finish that minimizes dead spots.

  • Water-Resistant After Cure

    Unlike our standard Conductive Paint which is rated for indoor use only, the Premium formula cures to a water-resistant finish. It stands up to humidity, condensation, and light moisture exposure — making it suitable for outdoor enclosures, junction boxes, and any environment where occasional moisture contact is expected.

  • Guitar & Instrument Cavity Shielding

    The go-to choice for musicians and luthiers. Line the body cavities of electric guitars, basses, and other instruments to eliminate single-coil hum, reduce noise pickup, and dramatically clean up your signal. One coat typically removes most of the noise; two coats and it's silent.

  • Safe, Non-Toxic & Easy Cleanup

    Despite its professional-grade performance, the formula remains water-based and non-toxic. Cleanup before cure is easy with soap and water. No harsh solvents, no special ventilation, and no PPE beyond basic precautions are required.

Applications

  • Guitar & Instrument Shielding: Eliminate hum and noise in electric guitar and bass cavities.
  • RF Enclosures & Faraday Shields: Line housings for RF-sensitive equipment, test chambers, and Faraday cages.
  • Outdoor Electronics: Protect enclosures and junction boxes in moisture-exposed installations.
  • Industrial EMI Shielding: Shield control panels, motor housings, and industrial electronics from interference.
  • Medical & Lab Equipment: Reduce EMI in sensitive diagnostic and measurement instruments.
  • PCB & Enclosure Prototyping: Rapid shielding for prototype evaluation before committing to machined housings.

Compatible Surfaces

  • Wood and MDF (guitar bodies, enclosure panels)
  • Metal and coated metal surfaces
  • Plastic and fiberglass
  • PCB substrates and drywall

Usage Guidelines

  • Apply on clean, dry surfaces — degrease metal substrates before painting
  • Allow each coat to fully dry before applying the next
  • For outdoor exposure, seal with a clear protective topcoat after cure
  • Full electrical and mechanical properties reached at 24 hours
